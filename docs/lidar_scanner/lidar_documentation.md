## STM32F407VET6 wiring

The 5-pin LiDAR lead does **not** plug directly into one STM32 USB-C socket.
On the Hiwonder ROS Robot Controller V1.2, use the `BT/UART2` connector for
the four power/UART wires and the `PC10` contact on the 26-pin expansion header
for the optional time-synchronization wire.

### LiDAR lead to STM32

The order below is the order shown in `Lidar_wiring_and_dimensions.png`, viewed
from the LiDAR wire-entry side:

| LiDAR lead | Function | STM32 connection | Physical location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Time synchronization | `PC10` GPIO/timer signal | 26-pin expansion header; use the contact marked `PC10` |
| 2 | RX | `PD5` / `USART2_TX` | `BT/UART2` connector |
| 3 | TX | `PD6` / `USART2_RX` | `BT/UART2` connector |
| 4 | Negative electrode | GND | Any `GND` contact on the 26-pin header |
| 5 | Positive 5V | +5V | `5V` contact on the 26-pin header |

UART is crossed in the normal way: LiDAR `TX` goes to STM32 `RX` (`PD6`), and
LiDAR `RX` goes to STM32 `TX` (`PD5`). The `BT/UART2` connector is the header
on the upper-right area of the controller, beside the buzzer and power switch
when the motor connectors are at the top. Use only its UART signal contacts;
take LiDAR power from the explicitly marked `5V`/`GND` contacts on the 26-pin
header. Do not use the
product-labeled `UART1` USB-C connector; it is reserved for the Rock64 host
link on `PA9`/`PA10`.

The time-synchronization connection is optional for ordinary serial scan data.
Leave it disconnected if synchronization is not being implemented, rather than
connecting it to an arbitrary UART pin.

### Firmware status

The current STM32 firmware initializes `USART2` on `PD5`/`PD6` at 9,600 baud
for the auxiliary/Bluetooth interface. The LiDAR documentation specifies
115,200 baud, 8N1. Before expecting data, the firmware must therefore change
USART2 to 115,200 baud, assign the port to the LiDAR, configure `PC10` if time
synchronization is needed, and add the STL-50B2 frame parser. Wiring alone does
not enable LiDAR scans in the current image.
